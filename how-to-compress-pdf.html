How to Compress a PDF Without Losing Quality

A 50 MB PDF is a headache — it will not fit in an email, takes forever to upload, and eats your storage. The good news: most PDFs can be made dramatically smaller without visibly losing quality. This guide shows you how to compress a PDF for free, in your browser, with no uploads and no software.

Why are PDFs so big?

The biggest reason is embedded images. A scanned document stores every page as a high-resolution image, and many PDFs carry photos at print quality. Text-only PDFs are tiny; image-heavy PDFs are not. Compression works by re-encoding those images more efficiently and lowering their resolution only where it is invisible to the eye.

How much can you shrink a PDF?

🪶
Light
~20–40% smaller
Gentle compression, near-perfect quality. Great for documents you will print.
⚖️
Balanced
~40–70% smaller
The best everyday choice for email and sharing. Quality looks the same on screen.
🗜️
Aggressive
~70–90% smaller
Smallest file possible. Use for storage and web uploads where maximum size matters.

How to avoid a huge PDF in the first place

  • Scan at 200–300 DPI instead of 600 — the file is 4× smaller for almost no visible difference.
  • Use black-and-white mode for text documents; color scans are much larger.
  • OCR text pages and keep them as compact, text-based PDFs instead of images.
  • Remove unused pages with the Split tool before sharing.
