How to Make a Scanned PDF Searchable (Free, No Upload)

If your PDF is a scan and Ctrl+F finds nothing, it does not contain text yet — only pictures of text. This guide shows you how to turn any scanned PDF into a searchable, copyable, selectable document in a few clicks, using free browser-based OCR. No software, no account, and nothing gets uploaded.

Why your scanned PDF is not searchable

A scanned PDF is really a stack of page images. The file looks perfect on screen, but it has no text layer at all. That is why you cannot search it, copy from it, or edit it, and why Google cannot read what is inside it. OCR — optical character recognition — reads the image and adds a hidden text layer, making every word searchable.

What you need

  • The scanned PDF on your computer, phone, or tablet.
  • A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
  • About a minute per 20–30 pages, depending on your device.

Step-by-step: make a scanned PDF searchable

1

Open PDFyre in your browser

Go to pdfyre.xyz. Everything runs on your device, so there is nothing to install and no file ever leaves your computer.

2

Add your PDF

Drop the file onto the OCR tool (or choose it from your files). It opens instantly — there is no upload bar to wait on.

3

Pick quality and language

Choose a quality mode (Balanced is a great default) and the document's language so the engine recognizes characters correctly.

4

Run OCR

Press the run button. You will see live progress, per-page confidence, and a preview of each page as it is processed.

5

Download your searchable PDF

When it finishes, download the file. Open it and press Ctrl/Cmd+F — every word is now searchable and selectable.

Tips for the best OCR results

  • Start from a clean scan. 200–300 DPI, flat pages, dark text on a light background.
  • Choose the right language. Wrong-language OCR turns accurate text into nonsense.
  • Use a higher quality mode for faded or small text; use a faster mode for simple, large print.
  • Skewed or rotated pages reduce accuracy — keep pages straight when scanning.

💡 Already OCR'd? Some files already have a text layer. PDFyre detects those pages and skips them automatically, so the process stays fast.
